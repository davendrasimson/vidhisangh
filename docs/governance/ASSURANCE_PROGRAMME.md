# Training, internal audit and management-review programme

## Training register

Training covers phishing and credentials, MFA/passkeys, client confidentiality, safe document handling, privacy requests, incident reporting, acceptable use and secure use of AI/research sources. Record participant, role, module/version, completion date, assessment result and acknowledgement in the controlled firm workspace.

Initial sessions are **not recorded as complete by this document**. The executive owner schedules onboarding training before privileged access and annual refreshers thereafter.

## Internal audit plan

| Period | Scope | Evidence sample | Reviewer requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q4 2026 | Access, change control, backups, malware and alerts | Users/roles, deploy commit, restore report, ClamAV state, failure-alert configuration | Person not operating the sampled control |
| Q1 2027 | DPDP/PIMS operations | Notices, consent events, requests, grievances, retention and supplier records | Privacy-competent reviewer |
| Q2 2027 | Secure development and vulnerability management | CI/SCA/SAST results, DAST, penetration-test findings and closure | Application-security reviewer |
| Q3 2027 | Continuity and suppliers | Restore drill, incident exercise, hosting/mail/storage agreements and exit plans | Independent reviewer |

Each finding records requirement, evidence, severity, root cause, owner, due date, corrective action and retest result. Overdue high findings are escalated to the executive owner.

## Quarterly management review

The executive owner chairs a recorded review covering:

1. prior actions and changes in legal, business, supplier and threat context;
2. risk register and any proposed risk acceptance;
3. incidents, alerts, vulnerabilities and penetration-test status;
4. availability, backup success and restore evidence;
5. privacy requests, grievances, retention and counsel advice;
6. access reviews, changes, audit findings and corrective actions;
7. supplier performance and planned integrations;
8. training completion, resources and improvement decisions.

Minutes identify attendees, evidence considered, decisions, action owners and dates. A meeting is not marked complete until the owner approves its minutes. The first formal management review remains to be held.
