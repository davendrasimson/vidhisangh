## Operating cadence

| Activity | Owner | Frequency | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Review risk register and treatment dates | Executive owner | Monthly | Updated register and decisions |
| Review privacy requests and grievances | Grievance Officer | Monthly | Request register and closure times |
| Check backup, SMTP, ClamAV and scheduled jobs | Technical owner | Weekly | Timer/service status and alert log |
| Restore exercise | Technical owner | Quarterly | Dated restore report |
| Supplier/sub-processor review | Executive + privacy owner | Quarterly and before onboarding | Register, DPA/terms and decision |
| Security/privacy awareness | Executive owner | At onboarding and annually | Attendance and acknowledgement |
| Internal control audit | Assigned independent reviewer | Twice yearly | Findings and corrective actions |
| Management review | Executive owner | Quarterly | Signed minutes and action owners |
| External penetration test | Independent tester | Annually and after major architecture change | Final report and retest evidence |

Document changes are reviewed through Git. Records containing personal data, contracts or confidential evidence must remain in the controlled firm workspace rather than this public code tree.
