# VidhiSangh user guide

Use **Help** in the top bar for the current page. The **light bulb** opens a short tip about what else you can do there. Both popups can be closed with Escape and leave the current form in place.

Open **Help & feature tour** in the navigation to search all articles or take the feature tour. The tour has Back, Next, Close and Finish controls and can be replayed at any time. It explains features without creating records or navigating away from your work.

Feature availability depends on your role, subscription and configured services. Ask your organisation administrator if a described action is unavailable. For account assistance, open **Support & grievance** from the top bar or account menu.

<a id="dashboard"></a>
## Dashboard

Start your day with an overview of your practice.

1. Review the dashboard cards and upcoming work.
2. Use the navigation to open matters, documents, calendar and billing.

**Tip:** The navigation also includes My timesheets, Bare Acts and your firm’s Knowledge Library.

<a id="matters"></a>
## Matters and custom forms

Create and organise cases, hearings and matter records.

1. Choose New matter, enter the case details and save.
2. Use Fetch from eCourts to import available case information when your provider is configured.
3. To customise forms, open Configure additional matter fields (the sliders icon). Add a section, then add fields and choose where they belong. This requires tenant management permission.

**Tip:** You can add custom fields and sections to matter forms, including sensitive fields. Ask your administrator if the configuration icon is missing.

<a id="matter-detail"></a>
## Matter workspace

Keep the case record and related work together.

1. Open a matter to review its details and available actions.
2. Use Research or Bare Acts to continue from the case’s acts and sections.
3. Review the matter’s hearings, team and linked work as your access allows.

**Tip:** Research and Bare Acts links carry case context, saving you from entering the same information again.

<a id="matter-portal"></a>
## Matter client portal

Manage the information shared through a matter’s client portal.

1. Review the portal settings and client access for this matter.
2. Choose the information to share before providing a client with their portal link.

**Tip:** The client portal provides a separate client-facing view of shared case information.

<a id="documents"></a>
## Documents and e-signatures

Draft, upload, organise and send documents for signing.

1. Create a draft using a built-in format, or upload a file in the document workspace.
2. For signing, open a file’s actions menu and choose Prepare for e-signature. Complete the recipients and signing setup before sending.
3. Open E-signatures to track documents sent by you and those awaiting your signature.

**Tip:** You can collect signatures inside VidhiSangh. You can also upload your firm’s Word templates and edit their fields for reuse.

<a id="draft-editor"></a>
## Document editor

Edit and export a draft in the full document editor.

1. Open a draft from Documents.
2. Use the editor toolbar to format the document and review its contents.
3. Check that your changes are saved before using Word export or Print/PDF.

**Tip:** Reusable Word templates let your firm preserve existing wording, tables and page setup.

<a id="calendar"></a>
## Calendar and hearings

Plan hearings and other practice events.

1. Select the date or view you need.
2. Create or open an event and review its time and related matter before saving.

**Tip:** Keep hearings connected to their matters so scheduling and case work stay together.

<a id="crm"></a>
## CRM and leads

Track enquiries through your intake pipeline.

1. Add an enquiry or open an existing lead.
2. Update its details and follow-up activity.
3. Open a lead when you are ready to convert it into a matter.

**Tip:** A lead can be converted into a matter from its detail page, avoiding a separate case-entry workflow.

<a id="lead-detail"></a>
## Lead details

Review an enquiry and move it toward an active matter.

1. Review the lead’s contact information and activity.
2. Use the conversion action when the enquiry becomes a case.

**Tip:** After conversion, the Matter link takes you directly to the resulting case.

<a id="clients"></a>
## Clients and KYC

Maintain individual and business client records.

1. Add a client and their contact information.
2. Open the client record to review consent, tax defaults and KYC.
3. Send the client a KYC form to confirm their details.

**Tip:** Clients can confirm their own details through a KYC link. Review their communication consent as well as their contact details.

<a id="client-detail"></a>
## Client record

Review a client’s information and related work.

1. Review contact information, consent and tax defaults.
2. Use the available KYC and related-record actions for this client.

**Tip:** GST and TDS defaults belong to the client record and help keep billing consistent.

<a id="billing"></a>
## Billing and invoices

Manage invoices and practice billing.

1. Open Billing and choose the relevant section.
2. Create or open an invoice and review its client, line items and totals.
3. Open an invoice to record receipts and review its balance.

**Tip:** Settings → Billing documents & branding lets authorised users customise firm identity, templates and numbering prefixes.

<a id="timesheets"></a>
## My timesheets

Record time spent on practice work.

1. Open My timesheets from the navigation.
2. Record time against the relevant work and review your entries.

**Tip:** Counsel rates are managed separately in Settings and can have effective dates.

<a id="invoice-detail"></a>
## Invoice and receipts

Review a billing document, balance and receipts.

1. Check the invoice details and current balance.
2. Under Payment & receipt, enter the amount, date, method and reference, then choose Record receipt.

**Tip:** Invoices include a verification QR code. Receipts retain the payment reference or UTR for reconciliation.

<a id="payments"></a>
## Payments

Review payment activity for your practice.

1. Open Payments and review the available payment records.
2. Check payment status and references against the associated invoice.

**Tip:** An invoice’s Payment & receipt area also supports recording bank, cash, cheque and online receipts.

<a id="research"></a>
## Legal Research

Find judgments and case information.

1. Enter a query and refine the available filters.
2. Open a judgment or case result to review its details and source material.

**Tip:** Start research from a matter’s Research link to carry over its case context.

<a id="judgment"></a>
## Judgment details

Read and review a research result.

1. Review the judgment details and available source text.
2. Check the original source and case context before relying on a result.

**Tip:** You can start a new search from a matter to include its acts and sections.

<a id="case"></a>
## Case details

Review information for a case identified by its CNR.

1. Review the case details returned by the configured provider.
2. Check available hearing and case information against the source.

**Tip:** Fetch from eCourts in Matters can help bring available case information into your practice.

<a id="knowledge"></a>
## Knowledge Library

Build a shared library of practice resources.

1. Choose the firm library view and search or filter resources.
2. Open a resource to read it, or add practice material with the available creation actions.

**Tip:** Save useful resources and use the saved-only filter to find them again.

<a id="bare-acts"></a>
## Bare Acts

Find legislation, guides and exact text.

1. Search or filter the acts library.
2. Choose Read exact text for the reader or Plain-language guide for an overview.
3. Review the status and version information for the Act.

**Tip:** Bookmark frequently used Acts and use the Bookmarks filter to return to them.

<a id="act-reader"></a>
## Act reader

Read legislation and inspect its context.

1. Open an Act using Read exact text.
2. Review the available text, source and version information.

**Tip:** The reader includes a Use in a matter area to connect legislation with case work.

<a id="news"></a>
## Legal News

Browse legal news and open articles.

1. Browse the available news and use the page’s filters.
2. Open an article to read its source.

**Tip:** Use Legal Research and Bare Acts alongside news when you need the underlying judgment or legislation.

<a id="network"></a>
## Professional feed

Share updates with your professional network.

1. Browse the feed and open relevant discussions.
2. Use the posting and interaction controls available to your account.

**Tip:** People, Groups and Messages provide other ways to continue a professional conversation.

<a id="people"></a>
## People

1. Search or browse people.

<a id="person"></a>

1. Review their professional information.

**Tip:** Your own profile can generate a QR visiting card and downloadable vCard.

<a id="groups"></a>

Find professional communities and discussions.

1. Browse the groups available to you.
2. Open a group to review membership and discussions.

**Tip:** Groups keep topic-specific discussions separate from your main feed.

<a id="group"></a>

1. Review the group description and membership.
2. Read or contribute to discussions using the actions available to your role.

**Tip:** Group actions depend on membership and your role in the group.

<a id="messages"></a>
## Messages

Continue direct professional conversations.

1. Select a conversation or start one using the available controls.
2. Review your recipient and message before sending.

**Tip:** Messages are available throughout the workspace through the application’s live messaging connection.

<a id="marketplace"></a>

1. Browse or search listings.

<a id="analytics"></a>
## Analytics

Review practice performance and trends.

1. Open Analytics and select the available reporting filters.
2. Review the displayed metrics in the context of the selected period.

**Tip:** A report’s date range matters: compare equivalent periods when assessing trends.

<a id="profile"></a>
## My profile and visiting card

Manage your professional identity.

1. Open My Profile from the account menu and review your details.
2. Under Digital visiting card, choose Show QR or vCard.

**Tip:** Your digital visiting card is generated from your professional details; update them before sharing it.

## Settings

1. Open a settings tile for the area you want to change.
2. Review and save changes where the page provides a save action.

**Tip:** Some organisation settings require an administrator. Available features also depend on your plan and configuration.

2. Use End today when an existing rate should stop applying.

**Tip:** Effective dates let you retain historical rates rather than overwrite them.
