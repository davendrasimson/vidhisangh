Section 508: Proceedings in wrong place. the at or area,

Chapter XXXVII — Irregular Proceedings

508. No finding, sentence or order of any Criminal Court shall be set aside merely on ground that the inquiry, trial or other proceedings in the course of which it was arrived passed, took place in a wrong sessions division, district, sub-division or other local unless it appears that such error has in fact occasioned a failure of justice.
