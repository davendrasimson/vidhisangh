Section 281: Rash driving or riding on a public way

Chapter XV — Of Offences Affecting the Public Health, Safety, Convenience, Decency and Morals

281. Whoever drives any vehicle, or rides, on any public way in a manner so rash or negligent as to endanger human life, or to be likely to cause hurt or injury to any other person, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine which may extend to one thousand rupees, or with both.
