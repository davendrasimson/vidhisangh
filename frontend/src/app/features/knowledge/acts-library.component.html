IPC or BNS? CrPC or BNSS? Understand the transition

The new criminal laws began their main operation on 1 July 2024. IPC → BNS covers offences; CrPC → BNSS covers procedure; Evidence Act → BSA covers evidence. Savings can keep earlier laws applicable to older incidents or pending proceedings. Do not simply replace old section numbers.

The original commencement excluded BNS section 106(2) and its corresponding BNSS schedule entry. Check subsequent notifications for the provision you need.
